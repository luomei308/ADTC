Ancestral Differences of Transcriptome and Clinical Utility in Cancers

Ancestral Differences of Transcriptome in Cancers (ADTC) is an interactive resource that enables users to explore ancestry-associated transcriptomic features with relevance to therapeutics and prognosis across multiple cancer types. Specifically, the database summarizes ancestry-related differences in long non-coding RNAs (lncRNAs) and post-transcriptional modifications (PTMs), including alternative splicing (AS), alternative polyadenylation (APA), and adenosine-to-inosine (A-to-I) RNA editing, among European (EUR), East Asian (EAS), and African (AFR) patients across 21 cancer types. In addition, ADTC highlights the distinct impacts of lncRNAs and PTMs on overall survival in different ancestral populations.
