Welcome to RNA Editing Section


The RNA Editing module includes two sub-sections:

Differential Analysis: Allows users to search by gene name to view ancestry-associated differences in A-to-I RNA editing among EUR, EAS and AFR across 21 cancer types.

Survival Analysis: Allows users to search by gene name to view ancestry-associated survival differences influenced by RNA editing.
