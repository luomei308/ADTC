Welcome to Alternative Splicing (AS) Section


The Alternative Splicing module includes two sub-sections:

Differential Analysis: Allows users to search by gene name to view ancestry-related splicing differences among EUR vs. AFR, EUR vs. EAS, EAS vs. AFR across 21 cancer types.

Survival Analysis: Allows users to search by gene name to view ancestry-associated survival differences influenced by AS among EUR, AFR,EAS across 21 cancer types.

📢 More details about data sources in ADTC can be found in Doucument
