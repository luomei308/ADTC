Welcome to lncRNA Section


The LncRNA module contains three sub-sections:

LncRNA Drug Target: Enables users to browse predicted drug response differences for lncRNA-targeting therapies currently under clinical investigation.

Differential Analysis: Allows users to search by lncRNA name to explore ancestry-associated differences in lncRNA expression across 21 cancer types, comparing EUR vs. AFR, EUR vs. EAS, and EAS vs. AFR.

Survival Analysis: Allows users to search by lncRNA name to explore ancestry-associated survival differences across 21 cancer types in EUR vs. AFR, EUR vs. EAS, and EAS vs. AFR.
